[B12]
Randomness doesn’t mean everything has to look evenly mixed. Imagine flipping a coin: getting five heads in a row may feel suspicious, but that particular sequence is just as likely as any other sequence of five flips.

In a long simulation, there are many chances for streaks, clusters, and other patterns to appear. Our brains are excellent at spotting them, even when no hidden rule caused them. A pattern can be real as an observation without being evidence that the process was rigged.
