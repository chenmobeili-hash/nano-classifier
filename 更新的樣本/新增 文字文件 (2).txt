\[
12870-5544-4410-1764+2100+630=3882。
\]

[B08]
平均数只告诉我们数据的整体水平，没告诉我们每个数离平均数有多远。

比如，一组是 5、5、5，另一组是 0、5、10，平均数都是 5。前一组每个数都一样，后一组差距却很大。知道这些差距，才能更完整地理解两组数据。

[B12]
Randomness can produce patterns because a pattern is simply one of the many possible outcomes. If you flip a coin ten times, a run of five heads may look surprising, but each flip can still be fair and independent.

Also, when you look at a lot of results, you have many chances to notice a streak or a cluster. Your brain is good at spotting those shapes, even when no one put them there. A pattern can be real in the results without having a special cause behind it.
