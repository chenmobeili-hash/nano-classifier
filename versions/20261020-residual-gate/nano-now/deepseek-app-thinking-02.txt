[B12]
Randomness can still create patterns because “random” doesn’t mean “perfectly mixed at every small scale.” In a simulation, each result may be independent, but in a short run you can get streaks, clusters, or repeated numbers by chance. Our brains are also very good at noticing those clusters and treating them as meaningful. If you run the simulation long enough, overall frequencies tend to even out, but local patterns can still appear.
