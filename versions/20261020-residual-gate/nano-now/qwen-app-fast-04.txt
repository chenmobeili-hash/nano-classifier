[B12] Randomness in a single event is unpredictable, but patterns show up when you look at many events together. Think of rolling a die: you can't guess the next number, but after a thousand rolls you'll see each face about one-sixth of the time. That's the law of large numbers—chaos at the micro level, order at the macro level. Simulations are the same. Each step is random, yet over time clusters, streaks, and shapes appear because probability has a kind of gravity. It's not that randomness is broken; it's that our brains are wired to notice structure, and structure is exactly what probability produces when given enough chances.
