[B08]
平均数只告诉你整体的平均水平，没有告诉你数据之间差多少。

比如，两组人的分数分别是“50、50、50”和“0、50、100”，平均分都是50。第一组人人一样，第二组却有人得零分、有人得满分。它们给人的印象自然不同。

所以，除了平均数，还要看看数据是否集中、最高最低差多少，以及有没有特别大或特别小的数。

[B12]
Randomness doesn’t promise an even-looking mix. It means individual outcomes are uncertain under the rules you’ve chosen.

Imagine flipping a fair coin ten times. HHHHHHHHHH has exactly the same probability as HTTHTHHTHT: each particular sequence has a chance of 1 in 1,024. The first one catches your attention because it has an obvious pattern. The second looks ordinary, so you probably wouldn’t notice it.

In a long simulation, there are many opportunities for streaks, clusters, and repeated sequences to appear. A fair coin also doesn’t “owe” you tails after several heads—the next flip still has a 50% chance of heads.

Most computer simulations use deterministic algorithms to generate pseudorandom numbers. Poor algorithms can introduce unwanted patterns, but even a good simulation will produce occasional streaks. To judge whether something is wrong, you need to check how often a pattern occurs compared with what chance predicts.
