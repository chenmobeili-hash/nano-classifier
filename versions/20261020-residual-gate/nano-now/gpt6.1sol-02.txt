[A12]
26个。先计首位为0、末位为1的情况。

4个1只能组成长度为1或2的连续段。段数为2、3、4时，段长安排分别有1、3、1种。4个0须分配到首段之前及相邻段之间，每处至少1个，对应分法分别为 \(\binom31,\binom32,\binom33\)。

因此这一类有：
1×3+3×3+1×1=13
个。反转字符串可与首1末0的情况一一对应，故共有 2×13=26 个。

[B12]
Random sequences naturally contain streaks, clusters, and repeats. For five fair coin flips, HHHHH and HTHTH each have a probability of 1 in 32. One looks like a streak and the other like an alternating pattern, but both are perfectly possible outcomes. A longer simulation gives these little patterns more opportunities to appear.

Our brains also notice interesting stretches and overlook ordinary ones. After seeing a sequence, it’s easy to find something that looks special—even if we never predicted it beforehand.

There’s another possibility: computer simulations usually generate randomness using algorithms. A poorly designed generator can introduce unwanted patterns. So spotting a pattern raises a useful question, but answering it takes more evidence: does that pattern occur more often than chance would predict across many runs?
