[B12] Randomness does not mean “no patterns.” It means that each outcome is not predictably controlled in advance. When many random outcomes happen, some clusters, streaks, or repetitions will appear simply by chance. For example, a fair coin can land heads several times in a row. That streak may look meaningful, but it does not prove the coin has changed. With enough trials, patterns are inevitable—even though no particular pattern was planned.
