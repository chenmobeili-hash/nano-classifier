为保证结论可靠，做完整动态规划：
n: 0 败；1 胜；2 败（只能到1）；3 胜（到0）；4 胜（到0）；5 胜（到2）；6 败（可到5、3、2：2败，故6胜）——修正：6取4到2，2败，故6胜；7：取1到6(胜)、3到4(胜)、4到3(胜)，全胜，故7败；8：取1到7(败)，故8胜；9：取1到8(胜)、3到6(胜)、4到5(胜)，故9败；10：取1到9(败)，故10胜；11：取4到7(败)，故11胜；12：取1到11(胜)、3到9(败)，故12胜；13：取4到9(败)，故13胜；14：取1到13(胜)、3到11(胜)、4到10(胜)，故14败；15：取1到14(败)，故15胜。

[B12]
Imagine flipping a coin and writing down heads or tails. If you flip it only a few times, you might get a streak of five heads and think something strange is happening. But randomness doesn't mean every outcome is equally spread out right away. Over many flips, the results tend to settle near half and half, yet the streaks still appear, because each flip doesn't know what came before. A computer simulation works the same way: it produces numbers that look random, and when you run it many times, clusters and patterns naturally show up. Those patterns aren't a sign the randomness is broken. They're just what chance looks like when you give it room to play out.
