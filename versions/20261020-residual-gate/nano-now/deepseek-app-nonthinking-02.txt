[B12] Imagine rolling a die many times and writing down the results. Randomness doesn’t mean “no patterns ever”—it means each roll is independent, but short runs can still look streaky. If you flip a coin 20 times, getting HHH or TTT somewhere isn’t shocking; there are many possible arrangements, and some of them contain clusters. Our brains are also pattern-hungry, so we notice the streak and ignore the ordinary rolls around it. A simulation can produce patterns by chance, just like real random events can. To judge whether a pattern is meaningful, you’d compare it with what random simulations typically produce, not just trust your first impression.
