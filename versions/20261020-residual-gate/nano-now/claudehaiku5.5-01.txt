[A10]
先手**必胜**。唯一能保证获胜的第一步是取 **1** 枚。

平均数只告诉我们“中间大概在哪”，却没告诉我们数据是怎样散开的。举个例子：A班和B班的考试平均分都是75分。A班每个人都在70到80分之间，成绩很接近；B班一半人50分，一半人100分，差距很大。两个班的“平均水平”一样，但真实情况完全不同。所以看数据时，除了平均数，还要看数据的分散程度（比如最高最低、范围或波动大小），才能真正理解它。

[B12]
Imagine you flip a coin 100 times and write down heads or tails. You'd expect it to look like a random mess, but simulations of randomness often produce patterns that look "too neat" or "too clumpy," and that's actually normal. Randomness doesn't mean every outcome is evenly spread out. Random things tend to form streaks and clusters just by chance. If you flip a coin enough times, you'll almost certainly see a run of five or six heads in a row, even though no single flip "remembers" what came before. Our brains are wired to see patterns, so we often read meaning into these streaks. A simulation shows us that the patterns come from the rules of chance itself, not from some hidden force making things happen on purpose.
