因为平均数只回答了“总量摊平后每人分到多少”，它丢掉了两个更要紧的东西：数据散得开不开，以及有没有特别大或特别小的值。
举个例子：甲班五人考 80、80、80、80、80，平均80；乙班五人考 100、100、60、60、80，平均也是80。可印象完全不同——甲班是“大家都稳”，乙班是“两极分化，有人满分有人刚及格”。
再比如两个小区月收入平均都是8000元：一个小区几乎人人7000到9000，另一个是多数人3000到4000、少数人月入十万。第二种情况里，“平均8000”对大多数住户毫无代表性，但数字照样是8000。
所以平均数是个压缩包：方便，但有损。要知道两组数据像不像，还得看它周围散得开不开、有没有极端值、数据是怎么分布的。这些信息一补上，两个“同一个平均数”就会给人完全不同的印象。

Think about what "random" actually promises. It doesn't promise the results will look messy, evenly mixed, or nicely spread out. It only promises you can't predict the next one. Everything else — the clumps, the streaks, the little clusters — is allowed, and with enough tries it's not just allowed, it's expected.

Flip a coin 100 times and you'll very likely see a run of five or six heads somewhere. People see that and think the coin is rigged or the simulation is broken. But how many different 100-flip sequences are there? Two to the hundredth power — more than the number of atoms in the observable universe. Every specific sequence is equally absurdly unlikely, including HHHHHH followed by nonsense. The one with the streak isn't special; it's just the one you noticed. HHHHHH and HTHHTH are equally improbable on paper, but only one of them feels like a message.

There's a second thing going on: your brain is a pattern detector that would rather be wrong than miss something. That's the same wiring that lets you spot a friend's face in a crowd. Hand it random data and it will happily find a face — a lucky cluster, a cold streak, a shape. The simulation isn't lying to you; you're just doing your job too well.

And a final twist: most computer "randomness" isn't random at all. It's a deterministic recipe — same starting number, same sequence, every time. That's how a simulation can be replayed exactly. Numbers from a formula can still pass every statistical test for randomness, because randomness isn't about how a sequence looks; it's about whether you could have predicted the next value in advance. Which is the real lesson: a pattern you can see afterward is not the same as a pattern you could have used beforehand.
