\[
\begin{aligned}
N={}&12870-6\times924-126\times35-84\times21\\
&+6\times10\times35+6\times5\times21\\
={}&3882.
\end{aligned}
\]
其中12870是总路径数；三个减项分别统计经过三个禁点的路径；两个加项分别统计同时经过(2,2)与另一个禁点的路径。(4,5)和(6,3)无法被同一条向右、向上的路径经过，因此其交集及三点交集均为0。
[A12]
26个。
先统计首位为1、末位为0的情况。若有 \(r\) 段连续的1，每段长度只能为1或2；4个1要求 \(r=2,3,4\)，其中长度为2的段数为 \(4-r\)。4个0也恰好分成 \(r\) 个非空段，因此数量为
\[
\sum_{r=2}^{4}\binom r{4-r}\binom3{r-1}
=3+9+1=13.
\]
把字符串反转，即一一对应到首位为0、末位为1的情况，所以共有 \(2\times13=26\) 个。

平均数把所有数加起来再平分，却没有告诉我们这些数之间相差多大。
比如，一组成绩是50、50、50、50分，另一组是0、0、100、100分，平均数都是50分。第一组的人水平相近，第二组却分成了两个差距很大的群体。所以，同样的平均数，可能藏着完全不同的情况。

[B12]
Randomness doesn’t promise an evenly mixed result. If a fair coin lands heads four times in a row, the next flip still has a 50% chance of being heads. The coin has no memory and no need to “make up” for the streak.
Patterns can happen by chance. Any particular sequence of ten fair flips has the same probability, but a sequence like ten heads grabs our attention much more than an ordinary-looking jumble. Run enough simulations, and you give surprising patterns plenty of opportunities to appear.
Computer simulations usually use pseudorandom numbers produced by an algorithm. A good generator can still produce streaks and clusters. To judge its quality, we look for systematic departures from what chance predicts across many results; one striking pattern alone tells us very little.
