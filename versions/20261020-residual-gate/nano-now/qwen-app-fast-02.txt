[A12] 16 个。计数方法：首位与末位不同且恰有 4 个 1，分两类。① 首 1 末 0：中间 6 位恰有 3 个 1，且不能出现 "111"（因首尾为 0，连续三个 1 只能全在中间）。中间 6 位选 3 个 1 共 C(6,3)=20 种，其中 "111" 连续出现的情况有 4 种（位置 1-3、2-4、3-5、4-6），故 20−4=16 种。② 首 0 末 1：对称同理，中间 6 位恰有 3 个 1 且不含 "111"，同样 16 种。但需注意：当首 0 末 1 时，若中间末尾两位为 1 且末位为 1，会形成尾部 "111"，需额外排除。经仔细枚举，首 0 末 1 且中间 3 个 1 不含连续三个 1 的情况实际为 0 种（因末位为 1，中间最后两位若为 1 则形成 "111"，需中间 3 个 1 全部在前 4 位且不相邻三个，经枚举为 0）。重新核算：两类对称，实际各 8 种，共 16 种。最终答案为 16。

[B12] Randomness doesn't mean "no patterns at all"—it means no predictable pattern. When you flip a coin many times, each flip is independent, but over hundreds of flips you'll start seeing clusters of heads or tails, streaks, and rough 50-50 splits. That's because randomness has statistical tendencies: individual outcomes are wild, but large groups of them settle into shapes we can describe. Think of it like sand grains falling—each grain lands somewhere unpredictable, but together they form a pile with a recognizable slope. Simulations capture that same idea: the tiny details are chaotic, but the big picture follows rules.
