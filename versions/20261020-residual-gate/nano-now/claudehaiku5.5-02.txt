[A12]
26。
计算方法：字符串反转后，首末位互换且保持1的个数与"无111"性质，因此"首0末1"与"首1末0"的数量相同，只需算前者再乘2。
"首0末1"时，中间第2到第7位含3个1、3个0，且不能出现111，也不能以11结尾（否则与末位1组成111）。用3个0划分出4个间隔，各间隔放的1的个数之和为3，每个间隔≤2，最后一个间隔≤1。
全部间隔≤2的方案有C(6,3)-4=16种，其中最后间隔为2的有3种，故合法为13种。
总数为 13×2=26。

[B12]
Imagine flipping a coin a hundred times and writing down the results. You'd expect a jumble, and most of the time you get one. But randomness isn't the same as evenness. Flip a fair coin long enough and you'll often see streaks of five or six heads in a row, which feel like the coin is "remembering" something. It isn't. Each flip is independent, but when you look at long sequences, chance naturally clumps. Simulations work the same way: a random process repeated thousands of times produces clusters, gaps, and shapes. Those shapes are real features of randomness, not signs that someone secretly arranged the outcomes. That's why a simulation can reveal patterns that feel meaningful even though nothing was designed to make them.
