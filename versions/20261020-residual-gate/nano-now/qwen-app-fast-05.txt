[B12] Randomness in a single trial is unpredictable, but when you repeat it many times, the law of large numbers kicks in: frequencies settle toward their expected values. So a simulation of coin flips will look messy up close, yet from far away you’ll see roughly half heads and half tails. That’s not a bug—it’s the pattern that randomness naturally produces when you give it enough room to breathe.
