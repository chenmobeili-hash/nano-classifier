[B12]
Randomness doesn’t mean every result has to look mixed up. It means the next outcome isn’t reliably predictable. A fair coin can still land heads five times in a row.

In fact, that exact sequence, HHHHH, has the same probability as HTHTH: 1 in 32. The first one just catches our attention because we recognize it as a streak.

When a simulation produces lots of outcomes, it creates lots of opportunities for streaks, clusters, and coincidences. We also tend to notice a pattern after seeing it, even though many different patterns could have caught our eye.

Some patterns can reveal a problem with a simulation. But a pattern alone isn’t proof: the useful question is whether it happens more often than chance would reasonably predict.
