Randomness doesn’t mean every short sequence looks perfectly mixed. If you flip a coin 20 times, streaks like HHH or patterns like HTHTHT can happen by chance. Your brain is very good at noticing those patterns and ignoring all the messy sequences around them. Also, small samples can look surprisingly lopsided; only over many trials do proportions tend to settle down. So a simulation can be genuinely random and still contain clusters, repeats, and apparent patterns—those are part of what randomness often looks like.
