共 3882 条。用容斥原理计算，记 P=(2,2)、Q=(4,5)、R=(6,3)。

总路径数 C(16,8) = 12870。
经过 P 的有 C(4,2)·C(12,6) = 6·924 = 5544 条。
经过 Q 的有 C(9,4)·C(7,4) = 126·35 = 4410 条。
经过 R 的有 C(9,3)·C(7,2) = 84·21 = 1764 条。
同时经过 P、Q 的有 6·C(5,2)·C(7,4) = 6·10·35 = 2100 条。
同时经过 P、R 的有 6·C(5,1)·C(7,2) = 6·5·21 = 630 条。
Q 和 R 无法出现在同一条路径上（从 (4,5) 到不了 (6,3)），所以经过 Q 且经过 R 的为 0 条，三点都经过的也为 0 条。
合法路径数 = 12870 − (5544 + 4410 + 1764 − 2100 − 630) = 12870 − 8988 = 3882。

平均数只告诉你“中间大概在哪”，却不告诉你数据有多分散。比如两个班平均分都是 70：一个班人人都在 68 到 72 分之间，另一个班一半人 40 分、一半人 100 分。平均数一样，但第一个班很整齐，第二个班两极分化，给人的感觉完全不同。所以除了平均数，还要看数据分散的程度和有没有极端值。

Here's the funny thing about randomness: it's lumpier than you'd expect. If you flip a fair coin 100 times, you'll almost certainly get a run of five or six heads in a row somewhere, and it'll look like the coin is "on a streak." Your brain is a pattern-detecting machine, so it flags that streak as meaningful, but it's exactly what randomness does.

Computer simulations add a second layer. Most of them don't use true randomness at all; they use pseudo-random number generators, which are deterministic formulas that churn out numbers that look random. A good one hides its structure really well, but a weak one can leave fingerprints: numbers repeating after a cycle, or points that line up in stripes if you plot them in 3D.

So when a simulation shows a pattern, ask two questions. Is this just the normal clumpiness of chance that my brain is over-reading? Or is it a real artifact of how the "randomness" was made? Sometimes it's one, sometimes the other, and figuring out which is half the fun.
