[B08]
想象两组学生的考试成绩,平均分都是 70 分。第一组所有人都考 70 分,整齐划一;第二组有人考 40 分、有人考 100 分,差距很大。平均数只告诉我们"中心在哪里",却没告诉我们数据是怎样分散的。两组数据的离散程度不同,所以给人的感觉也完全不同。因此只看平均数容易误判,还需要看分散程度(如极差、标准差)。

[B12]
Imagine flipping a coin again and again. Each flip is unpredictable on its own, yet over thousands of flips certain shapes start to show up: streaks, clusters, rough balance. That's the surprising part of randomness. Local chance doesn't stop global structure from appearing. A simulation just follows simple rules many times, and the patterns it produces come from the rules' repetition and the accumulation of chance, not from any hidden plan. So the order you see isn't a contradiction of randomness; it's one of its most interesting faces.
